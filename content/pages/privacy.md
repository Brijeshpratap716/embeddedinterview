title: Privacy Policy
desc: Privacy policy for EmbeddedInterview.com.
---
*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

**No accounts, no tracking cookies.** EmbeddedInterview.com does not require registration and does not set advertising or tracking cookies.

**Data stored in your browser.** Your progress (questions marked done, quiz best score, code you type in the practice editor) and your theme choice are saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

**Third-party services.** Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of the request. The "Run in Compiler Explorer" button sends the code in the editor to godbolt.org only when you click it; their privacy policy applies there. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security and operations.

**Email.** If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
