EmbeddedInterview.com is a free resource for engineers preparing for embedded software, firmware, BSP and Linux kernel interviews.

Everything here is written by working embedded engineers: interview questions with model answers phrased the way you would say them in an interview, coding problems with tested reference solutions, a multiple-choice quiz and in-depth articles.

## What makes it different
- **Embedded first.** Questions come from real firmware and driver interviews: interrupts, registers, RTOS, protocols, the Linux kernel, not generic web-developer trivia.
- **Answers that go one level deeper.** Each answer explains the limits and the follow-up the interviewer is likely to ask next.
- **Tested code.** Every practice problem's reference solution compiles cleanly with `-Wall -Wextra` and passes its tests.
- **No sign-up.** Your progress is stored in your own browser.
