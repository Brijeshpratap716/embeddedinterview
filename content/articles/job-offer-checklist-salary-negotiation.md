Before accepting a job offer, check more than salary and designation.

## Job offer checklist

- **Salary:** Compare company, location and level on [Levels.fyi](https://www.levels.fyi/), [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/) and [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/). Negotiate based on market value, not just your current CTC.
- **Layoff risk:** Check [Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/), [Fishbowl](https://www.fishbowlapp.com/) and [Layoffs.fyi](https://layoffs.fyi/). Verify rumours with credible news and ask about project funding. Future layoffs cannot be predicted with certainty.
- **Actual role:** Ask how much work involves coding, feature development, testing, support and documentation.
- **Compensation:** Compare base pay, bonus, RSUs, vesting and repayment conditions rather than first-year CTC alone.
- **Career growth:** Check manager, work culture, technical ownership and promotion opportunities.
- **Offer letter:** Review probation, notice period, termination, work location, visa and relocation if applicable.
- **Resignation:** Confirm the written offer, joining date and pending approvals before resigning.

**Example:** If your current salary is ₹22 LPA and the offer is ₹28 LPA, but similar roles pay ₹35–45 LPA, negotiate toward the market range.

An interview is a two-way evaluation. Choose a role that offers fair compensation, stability and meaningful work.
