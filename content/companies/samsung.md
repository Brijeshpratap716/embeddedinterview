## How the process usually runs

Samsung hires embedded engineers through several entities, and the process differs between them. A common pattern is an initial timed coding round, followed by technical interviews and an HR round. Samsung Semiconductor India Research (SSIR) often recruits on campus for a 6-month internship with conversion to a full-time role, with separate software and hardware tracks. For embedded developer roles, candidates describe resume screening, a written technical test, C programming questions, debugging tasks, hardware-interfacing questions and a behavioural round.

## Topics that come up most

- **Coding:** graph traversal (BFS/DFS), linked lists, arrays, medium-hard HackerRank-style problems → [Data Structures](../../topics/data-structures/), [Arrays & Strings](../../topics/arrays-strings/)
- **C and C++:** inheritance and polymorphism internals in C++ for senior roles → [C++ for Embedded](../../topics/cpp-embedded/)
- **OS concepts** → [Operating System Concepts](../../topics/operating-systems/)
- **Embedded and debugging:** hardware interfacing, debugging tasks → [Embedded C](../../topics/embedded-c/), [Debugging & Tools](../../topics/debugging-testing/)
- **Hardware track (SSIR):** digital circuits, Verilog basics, AMBA bus protocols → [Electronics & Hardware](../../topics/electronics-hardware/)

## Questions candidates reported (paraphrased)

- Graph problems solved with BFS or DFS.
- How do inheritance and virtual functions work internally in C++?
- Linked-list manipulation and OS concept questions.
- For hardware roles: flip-flops, AMBA protocol basics, predicting the output of a small digital design.

## How to prepare

1. Practise timed coding: 2–3 medium problems in 90 minutes, in C or C++.
2. Revise C++ object model basics (vtables, constructors and destructors) for senior roles.
3. Prepare clear explanations of your projects and the debugging you did on them.
4. Check which Samsung entity and track (software or hardware) you are interviewing for; the topics differ.
